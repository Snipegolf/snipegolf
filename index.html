Everything your sweep needs

Designed for the way clubs actually run sweeps.

From the bar staff scanning a QR code to the captain checking the standings on the back nine, every surface earns its keep.

Live leaderboards

Powered by ESPN data. Refreshes every 60 seconds during play with rank movement, tiebreakers and round-by-round picks.

Mobile entry

One link. Two golfers per bracket, tiebreaker, done. Searchable lists, no iOS pinch-zoom, 48-pixel tap targets.

Printable QR posters

Single-sheet A4 poster for the clubhouse notice board. Scan, enter, done. Branded with your club crest and tournament logo.

Auto WD swap

If a player withdraws before Thursday, SnipeGolf automatically swaps to the next available golfer in the same bracket. No spreadsheets at midnight.

Admin dashboard

Token-gated. Players, picks, payments, WD log, message blasts, lock entries — one screen, mobile-friendly.

GDPR-compliant

Built for the EU. Explicit consent, right-to-erasure, 30-day post-event retention. Hosted on Google's infrastructure under your club's control.

From zero to live in minutes

Three steps. No code.

1

We provision your league

Send us your club name, tournament and prize. We spin up a private league page, an admin link and a member entry link.

2

Members enter on their phone

Share the QR code or WhatsApp link. Players pick 2 golfers from each of 4 brackets and submit a tiebreaker.

3

Watch it play out live

The leaderboard updates every minute. Withdrawals are swapped automatically. The captain has the trophy waiting on Sunday.
