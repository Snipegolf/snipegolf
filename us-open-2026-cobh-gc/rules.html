The Open 2026 — Rules

Oakmont · June 2026 · Entry €10

How to play

  1. Pay €10 entry — Revolut revolut.me/badenimdn, cash in the shop, or on the sheet in the clubhouse.
  2. Enter online via the entry page. You get a personal email link to make (and edit) your picks.
  3. Pick 2 golfers from each of the 4 brackets (8 picks total).
  4. Add a tiebreaker score (predicted winning total, e.g. -12).

Key dates & times (BST)

  • Entries open: Thu 2 July 2026
  • Brackets locked in: already locked — no changes to the field except withdrawals
  • Withdrawal cutoff: pre-lock withdrawals auto-swap for the next available alternate. After the lock, a withdrawn pick scores your bracket leader + 5 (Missed Cut penalty).
  • Repicks / entries close: Wed 15 July, 22:00
  • Admin edits close: Thu 16 July, 08:00 (first tee)

Scoring

  • Your total = sum of your 8 golfers' scores relative to par (lowest total wins).
  • If your golfer misses the cut, is withdrawn, or is disqualified after the lock: they score the leader of their bracket + 5 (MC+5).
  • Live leaderboard updates every hour during the tournament.

Tiebreaker

  1. Closest predicted winning total (ties broken by absolute distance).
  2. Then: head-to-head across your 8 sorted picks.
  3. Then: alphabetical.

Prizes

Prizes to be confirmed — see the entry page for the latest.

Fair play

  • One entry per person. Duplicate names flagged and reviewed.
  • Picks are final at 22:00 on Wed 15 July — no exceptions.
  • Any dispute: contact Conor at the club.

Questions? Ping Conor in the clubhouse or reply to your entry email.
